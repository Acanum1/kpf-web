Delete your Bevora account

This page applies to the app Bevora (developer: Tobias Knoke). You can delete your account yourself in the app at any time or request deletion by email.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Bevora and go to Settings.
  2. Under “Account”, tap “Delete account”.
  3. Confirm the deletion. Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Request by email

If you can no longer access the app, write from your account’s email address to hallo@bevora.app with the subject “Delete Bevora account”. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm this to you by email.

What is deleted

  • your account with email address, password, display name and personal settings
  • the push tokens of your devices
  • households in which you are the only member, with pantry, shopping list, budget, history and symbols

What remains

  • In shared households, pantry, shopping list and history remain for the other members, but without your name.
  • Please also cancel running subscriptions in the App Store or on Google Play.
