No tedious typing. Bevora does the work and speaks up when it matters.

01

  • Or scan a barcode
  • Add best-before date and storage place

Remind

Features

One app for pantry, shopping and budget.

🧾

Receipts & eReceipts

Photo or PDF, done. Items, quantities and prices go straight into your pantry. Bevora learns along the way, even cryptic abbreviations like “MAOAM MIX”.

⏰

Best-before reminders

Know in time, not when it’s too late. For fruit and veg without a date, Bevora estimates freshness by type.

🛒

Shared shopping list

Everyone sees instantly what’s missing, with a push when something is added. You can dictate items too.

📦

Pantry by place

Fridge, freezer, cupboard: everything in its place. Bevora recognises frozen food and ice cream by itself.

🥑

Symbols that fit

Every product with its own picture, the same on iPhone and Android. Don’t like one? Pick another for the whole household.

💶

Spending at a glance

Monthly budget, previous months and who in the household paid for what.

🍳

Recipe ideas Plus

Suggestions based on what should be used up first.

🔔

Push for the household

Bundled notifications when someone shops or something expires soon. No flood of messages.

🔒

Data in the EU

Stored in Ireland, no ads and no selling of data. Every household only sees its own data.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What does Bevora cost?

Pantry, shopping list, household and reminders are free. Bevora Plus with AI receipt scan, AI recipes and budget history is a subscription for the whole household: €2.99 per month or €24.99 per year, for up to 6 adults and any number of children, with a 2-week free trial.

Which devices does Bevora run on?

iPhone and Android. All devices in a household see the same pantry, whatever phone someone has.

Which languages and countries?

The app is available in German and English. Bevora launches in Germany, Austria and Switzerland first; receipt recognition is optimised for supermarkets there.

Do I have to type in every item?

No. Just snap the receipt or scan the barcode. You can add the best-before date with a few taps.

Does Bevora stop food from expiring?

Bevora is a reminder aid, not a guarantee. Dates and reminders can be wrong or missing. Always check the shelf life and condition of your food yourself before eating it. Details are in the terms of use.

Is it about eating expired food?

No, quite the opposite: Bevora helps you use food before the best-before date is reached, so nothing has to go to waste in the first place.

Where is my data stored?

In a data centre in Ireland (EU). Every household only sees its own data, Bevora shows no ads and sells no data. More in the privacy policy.

Do children need an email address?

No. An owner shows a QR code, the child’s phone scans it and joins, with restricted rights.

Where can I get Bevora?

For Android on Google Play. The iPhone version is coming soon to the App Store. Questions and ideas are welcome at hallo@bevora.app.
