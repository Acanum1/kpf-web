2. What this is about

Bevora is an app that lets households manage their shared food pantry, a shopping list and their budget. We only process the data needed for this. There is no advertising, no tracking and no selling of data.

3. Which data we process

  • Account: email address, password (stored only in encrypted form), display name, personal app settings.
  • Household: name of the household, members and their roles (owner, member, child), invitations.
  • Pantry and shopping: products with quantity, shelf life, storage place, price and shop, shopping list, budget, recipes, history (who used or threw away what and when).
  • Photos of receipts and packaging: only if you use this feature. The photos are transmitted for analysis and not stored permanently.
  • Device data: a push token of your device so that notifications arrive.
  • Bevora Plus: whether and until when a subscription exists, which subscription (month/year), via which store and for which household it applies. We do not receive payment data such as credit card or bank account numbers – these are processed by Apple or Google.

The legal basis is the performance of the user agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). For optional features such as push notifications and AI analysis, it is also your consent through use (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time in the settings or by not using them.

4. Who sees the data

A household’s data is only visible to its members. Children do not see prices or the budget.

4a. Shared receipt dictionary

To improve receipt recognition for everyone, Bevora learns collectively: when you accept or correct an item from a receipt, we store only the supermarket chain (e.g. “REWE”), the text of the receipt line (e.g. “MAOAM MIX”), the product name and the product category – linked to your household so that each household has only one vote. If several households agree, Bevora suggests this name and category to all users. Other users only see the result, never which household or person voted. Prices, quantities, purchase dates and personal details are not shared. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in better recognition for all users (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If a household is deleted, its votes are deleted too.

4b. Shared product symbols

If you choose your own symbol for a product (e.g. 🥛 for “oat milk”), we store only the product name, a key derived from it and the chosen symbol – linked to your household so that each household has only one vote. If at least three households choose the same symbol by majority, Bevora also shows it to other users. Other users only see the symbol, never which household or person chose it. You can turn off these suggestions in the settings under “Product symbols”. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in fitting symbols for all users (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If a household is deleted, its symbols are deleted too.

5. Service providers we use

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc., USA): database, sign-in and server functions. Data storage location: EU, Ireland (Dublin data centre, region eu-west-1).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC, USA): delivery of push notifications.
  • Anthropic (Anthropic PBC, USA): AI analysis of receipt and packaging photos and recipe suggestions – only if you use these features. Only the photo or the product list is transmitted, not your name or email address. According to Anthropic, this data is not used to train AI models.
  • Open Food Facts (non-profit database, France): when scanning a barcode, only the barcode number is queried.
  • Apple / Google: on-device text recognition and speech recognition when dictating use your phone’s services. Subscribing to and paying for Bevora Plus is done via the App Store or Google Play under their privacy policies.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA): processing and verification of Plus subscriptions. We transmit a random account ID (not your email address and not your name), the ID of your household, the store’s purchase receipts and technical details such as operating system, app version and store country.
  • Resend (Resend, Inc., USA): sending the app’s emails, e.g. invitations to a household. The recipient address and the content of the email are transmitted.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA): hosting of this website. When you visit it, technically necessary access data (e.g. IP address) is processed.

For service providers in the USA, data is transferred on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or EU standard contractual clauses.

6. Children

Children under 16 can only be added by an owner of the household via QR code. This requires the consent of a parent or guardian (Art. 8 GDPR). Child accounts have no email address and no password; only the first name entered by the parents is stored. Children do not use AI and do not see prices. Parents can remove the child from the household at any time.

7. Storage period and deletion

We store your data as long as you have an account. We delete subscription data as soon as it is no longer needed for the subscription, unless statutory retention obligations apply. You can delete your account yourself under Settings → Account → Delete account. Households in which you are the only member are deleted with all their data. In shared households, the entries remain for the other members, without your name. How to have your account deleted without the app is described under Delete account.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR) as well as the right to withdraw consent. Just write to hallo@bevora.app. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Security

The connection between app and server is encrypted. Every household is separated from others by access rules in the database.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will inform you in the app.
